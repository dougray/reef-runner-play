A pirate run-and-gun. Run right through an island, a merchant ship, the hold and a pirate ship, fighting rival crews and their bosses and grabbing gold. Keyboard: arrow keys or WASD move and aim, Space or Z jumps, X, J or F fires (your blade swings when a sailor is close), P or Escape pauses, M mutes, C opens the Captain's Quarters from the title screen. On a phone: drag on the left side to move and aim, and use the JUMP and FIRE buttons. Areas, bosses, victories and your final score are announced.
