What ye're in for

Run and gun

Run, jump and shoot in eight directions through an endless voyage. Get close and your blade swings on its own.

Four rival crews

Crabs, toothy fish, bomb-throwing starfish, sharpshooting seashells, bald brutes and cannons across Palm Tree Island, a merchant ship, the hold and a pirate ship.

Four bosses

Every area ends with a boss, from the Big Guy to the Whale. Each warns you before it strikes.

Plunder

Rifles, a three-barrel pistol, a blunderbuss, grenadoes and a swivel gun. Spend run gold in the Black Market after each boss.

Your pirate

Bank your gold in the Captain's Quarters for coats, hats, outfits, blades, guns, classes, a parrot or a monkey. Play as the captain or the she-captain.

Yours to keep

Everything runs on your device and saves there too. Install it from the browser and play with no internet.

How to play

KeyboardArrows or WASD move and aim · Space or Z jumps (tap for a hop, hold for a full jump) · X, J or F fires · P pauses · M mutes · C opens the Captain's Quarters from the title
ControllerXbox or PlayStation controller: stick or D-pad moves and aims · A jumps · X or B fires · Y opens the Captain's Quarters from the title · Menu pauses
PhoneUpright or sideways. The D-pad on the left moves and aims; tap JUMP (tap again in the air to double jump) and hold FIRE on the right. Menus are all tap.
TipsCrouch under cannonballs. Jump over shockwaves and rolling kegs. Down + Jump drops through docks. Every 100 gold in a run is an extra life.
InstallOn a phone, use your browser's Share or menu button and choose Add to Home Screen. On a computer, look for the install icon in the address bar.
