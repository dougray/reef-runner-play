Join the official beta

Reef Runner, Jolly Rogue and Cloud Dancer are on their way to the App Store and Google Play. Play them first, and tell us what breaks.

On iPhone or iPad

The beta runs through TestFlight, Apple's free app for testing apps before they reach the App Store.

  1. Install TestFlight from the App Store.
  2. On the same iPhone or iPad, tap a game below.
  3. Tap Accept, then Install. The game appears on your home screen, and TestFlight keeps it up to date.
  • Reef RunnerJoin on TestFlight
  • Jolly RogueJoin on TestFlight
  • Cloud DancerJoin on TestFlight

On Apple TV

Each game's beta includes its Apple TV version. Play with the Siri Remote or a game controller.

  1. Join the game's beta on your iPhone or iPad first (above), signed in with the same Apple Account as the TV.
  2. On the Apple TV, install TestFlight from the App Store and open it.
  3. The games you joined are listed there. Choose one and select Install.

On Android

The Android versions are in a Google Play closed test, which needs your Google account on the tester list.

  1. Email dugcanlift@gmail.com with the Google account on your Android phone.
  2. Once you're on the list, open each game's test link on that phone and tap Become a tester: Reef Runner, Jolly Rogue, Cloud Dancer.
  3. Install the game from the Play Store link that follows.

Google asks new games to be tested for 14 days before they go public, so please keep them installed for two weeks.

No phone handy?

All three play right in your browser: Reef Runner, Jolly Rogue and Cloud Dancer.

Tell us what breaks

On iPhone, iPad and Apple TV, take a screenshot and TestFlight offers to send it as beta feedback. Or email dugcanlift@gmail.com with what happened and on which device.
