Support

Help with Reef Runner on iPhone, iPad, Android and the web.

How to play

  • Sail up: tap SAIL, or tap anywhere on the sea. Space, ↑ or W on a keyboard.
  • Fire: tap FIRE, and hold it to keep firing. F, X or → on a keyboard.
  • Pause: the pause button in the top corner, or P / Esc. The game also pauses when you leave the app.

Sink British frigates (1 hit) and Spanish galleons (3 hits), grab treasure chests and rum barrels, ride out the storms from reef 20, and take on HMS Invincible at reef 40.

No sound?

  • Check the speaker button in the top corner: if it shows 🔇, tap it to turn sound back on.
  • Turn the volume up with your device's volume buttons while the game is open.

Turning the device

The sea reshapes itself for portrait or landscape. Turning the device mid-run pauses the game so you don't lose your run; tap to carry on.
