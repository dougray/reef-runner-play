Steer your pirate ship between storm clouds and sandbars, and sink the King's navy.

Set sail
Free. Plays right here in your browser, nothing to download.

How to sail

Your ship always sails forward. Keep tapping to stay afloat between the clouds and the sand, and fire your bow cannon at anything flying a navy flag.

Keyboard and mousePhone or tablet
Sail up Space ↑ W or left-click The SAIL button, or tap anywhere on the sea
Fire F X → or right-click The FIRE button. Hold it to keep firing
Pause P or Esc The pause button in the top corner

What's out there

Cannonballs fly straight through storm clouds but bury themselves in sandbars, so a sandbar is cover for you and for them.

  • British frigate

    Quick and nosy. It follows your height and fires one aimed shot at a time. One hit sinks it, for 5 points.

  • Spanish galleon

    Slow, tubby and hard to sink. It takes three hits and fires fans of three shots. Worth 15 points.

  • HMS Invincible

    A three-deck man-o'-war waiting at reef 40. When its gun decks glow, find the gap in the wall of shot. Worth 100 points.

  • Storms

    They roll in at reef 20. Rain, lightning and gusts that shove your ship around, but every reef counts double.

  • Treasure and rum

    Chests are worth 10 points. A barrel of rum loads your cannon for five seconds of rapid fire.

Two ways to play

  • In your browser

    Choose Set sail. Nothing to install, and it works on phones, tablets and computers.

  • From your home screen

    iPhone and iPad: open the game in Safari, tap Share, then Add to Home Screen.

    Android, or Chrome and Edge on a computer: open the game and choose Install app from the menu.

    Once installed it plays with no internet at all.

No accounts, no ads and no tracking. The game runs entirely on your device, and your best score never leaves it.
