A pirate airship shooter. Four skies. Four bosses. Clouds to hide in.

CAST OFF

Free. Plays in your browser on computers and phones, and installs to work offline. No ads, no accounts, no tracking.

What ye're in for

Fly free

Steer anywhere in the sky with a thumb or the arrow keys. Your broadside cannons fire on their own; FULL STEAM rams anything in your way.

Hide in the clouds

Duck into a cloud bank and the navy loses its aim. So do you. In the Storm Wall, watch for lightning.

The King's Aerial Navy

Gyrocopters, zeppelins, balloon mines, flak towers, sky pikes and cloud lurkers across four skies.

Four bosses

HMS Thunderer, the Tempest Engine, Fort Sovereign and the Brass Kraken. Each warns you before it strikes.

Plunder

Chain shot, a harpoon, rockets and a Gatling gun. Spend run gold in the Sky Market after every boss.

Your airship

Bank your gold in the Hangar for envelope colours and patterns, sails, hull paint, a starting gun and a parrot.

How to play

KeyboardArrows or WASD steer · the cannons fire on their own · Space dashes on FULL STEAM · P pauses · M mutes · H opens the Hangar from the title
PhoneDrag anywhere to steer (the ship follows your thumb's movement, so your thumb never hides it). Tap FULL STEAM to dash. Menus are all tap.
TipsHide in clouds from aimed fire. Shoot balloon mines near the navy. Coal refills steam; a wrench mends a heart. Every boss gives an extra life.
InstallOn a phone, use your browser's Share or menu button and choose Add to Home Screen. On a computer, look for the install icon in the address bar.
