Cloud Dancer

Support

Help with Cloud Dancer on iPhone, iPad and Android.

How to play

  • Steer: put a thumb down anywhere and drag. The airship follows your thumb's movement rather than sitting under it, so your thumb never hides it.
  • Fire: the cannons fire on their own.
  • FULL STEAM: tap the round button to dash forward. You can't be hurt during the dash and you ram anything in the way. It uses steam, which refills over time.
  • Pause: the pause button in the top corner. The game also pauses when you leave the app.

Fly through the Reef Skies, the Storm Wall, the Sky-Forts and the Ceiling of the World; each ends with a boss. Beat it for an extra life and a visit to the Sky Market. Gold you pick up goes into your chest when the run ends; spend it in the Hangar.

Tips

  • Hide in a cloud bank and aimed shots stop following you.
  • Shoot balloon mines when the navy flies close to them.
  • Coal refills your steam; a wrench mends a heart.
  • Watch for the flickering column in the Storm Wall: lightning strikes there next.

No sound?

  • Check the music button in the top corner and tap it if the game is muted.
  • Turn the volume up with your device's buttons while the game is open. On iPhone, sound starts after your first tap.

Lost your airship?

Your airship, gold and unlocks are saved on your device only, so deleting the app or moving to a new phone starts fresh.
